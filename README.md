# Siegeline privacy policy

The privacy policy for the Siegeline game (Android and iPhone), published with GitHub Pages:

**https://hishamhikami.github.io/siegeline-privacy-policy/**

Edit `index.html` to change it. Use the link above as the privacy policy URL in Google Play
Console (App content → Privacy policy) and App Store Connect (App Privacy → Privacy Policy URL).

Terms of service (a sub-page, `terms/index.html`):

**https://hishamhikami.github.io/siegeline-privacy-policy/terms/**

Use it as the Terms of Service / EULA link in App Store Connect and Google Play.
