Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

In short: Siegeline does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection. Everything it saves stays on your device.

This policy explains how the mobile game Siegeline (the "app"), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store, handles information. It applies to all versions of the app.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect personal information of any kind: no name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports.

The Android version requests no permissions at all, including no internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, the app saves a small amount of data only on your device:

  • your settings (sound, controls, display and vibration options);
  • your mission progress, best scores, medals and service record;
  • your chosen weapon loadout;
  • a checkpoint of a mission in progress, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your device's own backup service (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include it in backups under that service's own terms.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Advertising, analytics and third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no social features and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. There are no in-app purchases.

Google Play and the Apple App Store, through which you download the app, operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information such as download statistics or crash reports that you allow them to collect. Those services are not controlled by us.

Children

Siegeline contains realistic combat violence and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

Security

Since no information is transmitted or stored outside your device, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can remove all data saved by the app at any time by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact the developer using the email address shown on Siegeline's Google Play or App Store page, or open an issue here.
