Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

In short: the Siegeline game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection. Everything it saves stays on your device. If you sign up for launch news on our website, we keep only your email address and phone type, to email you about Siegeline.

This policy explains how the mobile game Siegeline (the "app"), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store, handles information. It applies to all versions of the app.

Information the game collects

None. The app does not collect personal information of any kind: no name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports.

The Android version requests no permissions at all, including no internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, the app saves a small amount of data only on your device:

  • your settings (sound, controls, display and vibration options);
  • your mission progress, best scores, medals and service record;
  • your chosen weapon loadout;
  • a checkpoint of a mission in progress, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your device's own backup service (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include it in backups under that service's own terms.

Launch news sign-ups (our website)

On Siegeline's website you can sign up to hear when the game launches. If you do, we keep:

  • your email address;
  • your phone type (iPhone or Android), so we tell you about the right version;
  • when you agreed to this policy and the Terms of Service, and which version you agreed to;
  • if you came from a link that names its source (for example a post on a forum), that source.

We use this only to email you news about Siegeline, such as its launch. It is stored on our own server, is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers, and is not used for tracking. Our emails are delivered by an email delivery service (Brevo), which handles your address only to deliver them for us. We keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, which deletes your details straight away. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete them using the contact details below.

The sign-up page uses no analytics or advertising cookies. It sets only a security cookie that protects the form from forged submissions. Like any website, our server briefly sees your IP address while serving the page, and uses it to limit repeated sign-ups; we don't store it with your details.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with anyone. The game collects none, and the sign-up list above is used only by us.

Advertising, analytics and third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no social features and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. There are no in-app purchases.

Google Play and the Apple App Store, through which you download the app, operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information such as download statistics or crash reports that you allow them to collect. Those services are not controlled by us.

Children

Siegeline contains realistic combat violence and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

Security

The game transmits nothing and stores nothing outside your device. The sign-up list is kept on our own server, reached only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and only the developer can access it.

Your rights

The game holds no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete from it. You can remove all data saved by the app at any time by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings. If you signed up for launch news, you can unsubscribe from any email, or contact us to see, correct or delete your details.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact the developer using the email address shown on Siegeline's Google Play or App Store page, or open an issue here.
